10.  Why do we use media queries?
It helps to reshape the design depending on the size of the screen it will be presented on because otherwise a desktop sized 
website would look weird on for instance, a phone sized screen. 




11. What is the main difference between a serif and a sans-serif font?
Serif fonts are more decorative in style while sans-serif fonts are the same but without the added (serif) parts of the letters that
make it appear less simple and more fancy.
